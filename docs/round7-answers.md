# Round 7 questionnaire: the «Контент-план» module (2026-09-29)

A new 99 UAH module: 30-day content plan for the business's channels, built from real panel data. The agreed result goes into ARCHITECTURE.md.

## Block 1: basics
- K01 generation method: not answered — asked again in block 2 (K21)
- K02 name: «Контент-план»
- K03 replaces the planned «AI Контент» module in the catalogue: yes
- K04 channels (switches): Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, site, Telegram channel, YouTube Shorts, Viber community
- K05 a channel switch means only «plan for this channel» + a profile link (no account connection)
- K06 period: 30 days, refreshed weekly for the following days
- K07 default frequency by channel (e.g. Instagram 4 posts + daily stories, TikTok 3, Facebook 3, site 1)
- K08 rhythm: Light / Normal / Active + custom
- K09 brand voice: short setup (tone, «ти»/«ви», words to avoid)
- K10 language: Ukrainian
- K11 business brief: short 5-question form when the module is enabled
- K12 audience: asked + refined from data (cities, repeat clients, wholesale)
- K13 each idea explains «why this» from the data: yes
- K14 new business without data: ideas from the category and catalogue, getting more precise with data
- K15 available in the trial like other modules: yes
- K16 menu: «Контент» in the Робота group (locked until bought)
- K17 permission: new «Контент» permission, on by default for marketers
- K18 «Сьогодні запостити» card on Home: yes
- K19 morning Telegram reminder with a ready text: yes
- K20 honesty: strict — quotes only from real reviews, only real figures

## Block 2: data for ideas
- K21 generation: rules and templates now (free), AI later
- K22 data sources: best sellers, new products, low stock («останні 3 шт.»), overstock, reviews (esp. with photos), discounts (old price), holidays and seasons, channels that bring buyers, ad ROI, wholesale clients, sleeping clients
- K23 holidays: Ukrainian holidays + commercial dates (14.02, 8.03, 1.09, Black Friday, New Year), prepared 1–2 weeks ahead
- K24 business's own dates (shop birthday, collection launch): yes
- K25 overstock: only a post idea, no discount suggestion
- K26 where a discount is suggested (e.g. wholesale): respects cost price, never below the business's minimum margin
- K27 wholesale: «для оптових покупців» posts + wholesale discount from N items (setting)
- K28 automatic wholesale price in ONEKNIGHT: later
- K29 sleeping clients: «ми скучили» posts + reminders about new products; list of sleeping clients to call
- K30 reviews with photos: repost (the creative already exists) + «дякуємо»
- K31 ads that paid off: «повторіть формат / товар»; not paid off: «змініть»
- K32 evergreen ideas without data (behind the scenes, packing, production, team, tips by category): yes
- K33 content mix: sales 40% · usefulness 30% · trust 20% · fun 10%
- K34 the same product at most once per 7 days per channel
- K35 product flags «Просувати» / «Не просувати»: yes
- K36 searches without results → ideas: no
- K37 buyers' cities → ideas: no
- K38 «Акція» object in ONEKNIGHT (name, products, discount, dates) → the plan adds announcement, reminder, «останній день»: yes
- K39 data changed: refresh future ideas only; accepted ones stay
- K40 photos: catalogue product photos + «що зняти» brief for new shots

## Block 3: the idea card
- K41 card contents: channel, date and time, format (post / stories / reels / carousel / article), goal, why (from data), shot brief, text draft, call to action, hashtags, product link with UTM, products with photos
- K42 text drafts: 2 variants (short and long)
- K43 reels / TikTok / Shorts: the idea in one sentence (no shot-by-shot script)
- K44 3 hook options for the first 3 seconds of a video
- K45 stories: 3–5 frames + which stickers (poll, question, link)
- K46 carousel: 5–8 slides, heading and photo per slide
- K47 site content: article topic + outline, site banner text, product collection «до свята»
- K48 banner from the plan → into the site banner widget with show dates in one click: yes
- K49 Telegram channel / Viber community: short post + photo + link button
- K50 one idea adapted to several channels (main channel + «також для …»): yes
- K51 hashtags: 5–10 (category + product + city/Ukraine + the business's own tag)
- K52 posting time: general best hours per channel first, then from the hours of social-media visits to the site
- K53 automatic UTM link in every idea: yes
- K54 photo brief: one sentence
- K55 phone shooting tips: no
- K56 alt text: no
- K57 emoji in drafts: by brand voice
- K58 idea card buttons: copy text + hashtags, download product photos, edit text, «Інша ідея», «Опубліковано», send to my Telegram
- K59 «Зберегти як шаблон» for favourite ideas: yes
- K60 «+ Своя ідея» on any day: yes
